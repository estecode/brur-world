# Resumable OSM source-cache finalization

Issue: #207

The Sweden source-cache builder keeps the authoritative PBF as the only source of truth, but preserves a verified PKL spool after a completed source scan until route OSM caches and the cache manifest publish successfully.

A resumable spool is valid only when its completion marker matches the exact source identity, requested stale route versions, source-scan counts, and the size/SHA-256 of every spool PKL file. Missing, partial, stale, changed, or corrupt spool state fails closed and is rebuilt from the authoritative source.

Route finalization also bounds retained node-bucket file handles with a small LRU so multi-route finalization cannot retain hundreds of descriptors at once.
